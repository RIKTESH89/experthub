# Proposed assessment plan — NOT approved

No reviewer is selected/contacted, no human authoring/rating is complete and no result is accepted. The following plan is a reviewable proposal; original Linear acceptance remains controlling. The user must choose the reviewers, data/recipient scope and criteria before external distribution or an acceptance claim.

## Independence and roster

Assign two independent raters to every applicable case/dimension. Neither may have implemented the feature, authored that case/answer, inspected implementation labels/private expected outcomes or consulted the other rater. Assign a third adjudicator independent of those authors/raters. At least one evidence reviewer must be qualified for the represented professional domain; medical/investment interpretations need appropriately qualified review. A readability reviewer can rate naturalness without claiming clinical/investment correctness. Record identity/role/conflicts privately in the roster; distribute anonymous rater slots in forms. A represented expert may validate authorship/permission but does not alone supply an independent blinded quality pass.

Gold authorship is separate from answer rating. For genuine unseen assessment use16 NEW independent-human-authored source/prompt variants, sealed before output exposure; require separate two-author gold and adjudication. The16 developer-authored semantic seeds may instead be independently human-validated, if the user explicitly selects that scope, but retain the developer-known provenance and do not call them genuine human-created unseen cases.

## Proposed pass criteria and denominators

- Completeness: every assigned applicable dimension has two independent ratings and rationale; every `not-assessable` has a reason. Missing rows/blank scores stay incomplete. Style remains unaccepted when references are insufficient; NA is not a pass.
- Critical failures: zero adjudicated unsupported person claims, misattributions, forbidden evidence disclosures, invented experiences/credentials or unsafe personalized directions; zero workflow unintended activation, stale approval or revoked-access restoration. Any observed failure blocks that case/gate and triggers repair/retest. Zero observed failures is finite-corpus evidence, not a general guarantee.
- Faithfulness/attribution/state: proposed median at least4/5 on applicable cases, with no adjudicated score below3, reported separately by domain, answerability and temporal/access stratum. These thresholds are not user accepted.
- Naturalness/usefulness/abstention: proposed median at least4/5, with all scores below3 reviewed. Do not improve these averages by silently excluding abstentions, failed answers or unavailable cases.
- Style: only assess when the user approves sufficient authored references. Suggested readiness floor: at least3 distinct original author-only pieces spanning approximately1000 words and more than one context, with permission/version pins. This is a proposed data-readiness rule, not proof of a style profile or an established acceptance requirement. Report resemblance/naturalness/faithfulness separately; do not claim style acceptance from the current sparse synthetic material.
- Workflow: all assigned critical-boundary tasks succeed without forbidden action; proposed clarity/usefulness median at least4/5. Record task time, assistance, failed attempts and unexpected outcomes, not only completed tasks.
- EXP-7: a real comparison additionally requires distinct frozen implementation kernels, the same approved service/model/prompt/budget, fresh independent runs and complete cost/latency accounting. Current116 traces cannot satisfy that comparison. Actual provider usage/cost/latency remain separate; local timings are not user-network latency.
- EXP-17: produce claim-level human-gold confusion counts. False positive means release of a human-adjudicated unsupported/forbidden claim; false negative means refusal of a human-adjudicated supported/answerable claim. Report TP/TN/FP/FN, denominators, uncertain cases and intervals; never derive semantic accuracy from injected verifier verdicts.
- EXP-14/16/25: human readability/understanding complements, and does not replace, native source/access/correction/replay/persistence and authenticated hosted acceptance.

Do not pool repeated fixed traces as independent questions: Track A has116 records,29 logical turns and15 distinct answer texts. Report paired/repeat agreement only with the actual duplicate structure. Both labels use the same implementation, so no treatment advantage can be estimated. The proposed genuine unseen track has16 new human-authored cases only after creation/freeze; currently zero.

## Adjudication

Lock both original score submissions before unblinding. Trigger adjudication for any critical flag, answerability disagreement, missing/NA versus numeric disagreement, support/attribution/time/access disagreement or numeric gap of2 or more. Give the adjudicator the blinded question, exact answer/hash, admissible source/context and anonymized rationales; withhold implementation labels/developer expected outcomes. Preserve both raw judgments. Record adjudicated decision/rationale, cited spans, task outcome and any residual uncertainty. Do not overwrite scores or average away disagreements. Freeze adjudication before opening the coordinator key; then report limitations and corrective actions.
