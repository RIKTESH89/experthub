# EXP30 policy and operations review drafts

REVIEW DRAFTS ONLY — not approved policy, legal advice, binding terms, or published public routes. Do not link these as active policies. No compliance certification is asserted. Owner and qualified legal review are release prerequisites.

## Privacy notice working text

ExpertHub provides expert content and AI-generated conversations. Account identifiers, expert applications, supplied expert content, conversations, feedback, subscription/payment references, and operational logs may be processed to provide the relevant features. AI answers can be inaccurate. Avoid entering sensitive or third-party confidential information.

Before publication, the operator must validate a complete data inventory and name the legal entity, contact address, applicable jurisdictions, processing purposes and legal bases where applicable, data recipients/providers, international transfers, user rights/request channel, cookie/storage behavior and consent requirements. Audit actual behavior rather than promising deletion or anonymity that has not been implemented. Define retention separately for accounts, rejected applications, source documents, chat history/memory, feedback, billing/event receipts, logs and backups. Define how access, correction, export and deletion requests are authenticated and fulfilled, including backup and payment-record exceptions. No fixed retention period or provider data-use promise is approved here.

## Terms working text

A Digital Twin is an AI representation informed by expert-provided content and settings. It is not the expert and its generated answers are not necessarily reviewed or endorsed by that expert. Verify important answers and cited sources. Content coverage and recall are limited. The operator must approve eligibility, permitted use, expert content rights/licensing, source consent, moderation/reporting, account suspension/appeal, service availability, liability/disclaimer scope, dispute forum and applicable law before release. Do not present the system as a complete replica or certified professional adviser.

For paid access, show the exact plan, currency, billing interval, applicable taxes/fees and renewal terms before payment. Payment checkout completion alone is not verified access confirmation. Approve cancellation effective dates, paid-period access, plan-change handling and support expectations against implemented payment behavior and real sandbox acceptance. No uptime, accuracy or unlimited-use guarantee is approved here.

## Refund review draft

Publication blocked pending owner decisions: eligibility/events, request deadline and channel, duplicate or unrecognized charge handling, failed activation/service outage handling, partial-period treatment, payment processing fees, expected decision/payment timeframes and jurisdictional consumer rights. Do not use “no refunds” or promise guaranteed refunds without approved policy and operational ability to fulfill it. Prepare a reviewer workflow that checks a verified charge/reference and access timeline with minimum necessary data; do not request card details in chat. Any actual refund requires separately authorized provider action. This lane adds no refund algorithm.

## Incident playbook review draft

Proposed workflow for approval: intake a report; record timestamps and minimum necessary evidence; assess impacted tenants/data; contain using an authorized runbook; preserve relevant logs; assign an incident lead; verify scope and remediation; approve communications; restore carefully; complete a review with follow-up owners. Do not run migrations, revoke keys, alter production or send notices under this draft. Security boundaries and containment mechanisms are owned by EXP27.

Approve incident lead/backup, escalation channel, severity thresholds, on-call coverage, acknowledgement targets, provider liaison, breach assessment/notification obligations and authority to communicate. Establish a private log with access/retention rules. Customer updates should distinguish known facts, uncertainty and next update time; never claim a breach/compliance outcome without evidence.

## Bounded owner decision list (8 groups)

1. Operator identity, jurisdiction, legal/support/privacy contacts and responsible approvers.
2. Data inventory, provider processing/transfer terms and consent/cookie requirements.
3. Retention and deletion/export/correction procedures for each data class and backups.
4. Expert/content consent, licensing, eligibility and moderation/appeals.
5. Price/currency/tax disclosures, recurring renewal, cancellation timing/access and plan changes.
6. Refund criteria, deadlines, fees, request channel and fulfillment timelines.
7. Incident ownership, escalation/severity, coverage and communication authority.
8. Confirmed production public origin and release approval after Google/Razorpay sandbox, legal, accessibility and operations acceptance.

These decisions are unresolved; none is silently selected by this implementation.
