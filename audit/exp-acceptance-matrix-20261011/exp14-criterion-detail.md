Priority before any quality closure: independently author and seal multi-domain source/time relevance gold; label whether exceptions are applicable vs merely documented; include lexical/no-anchor paraphrases, competing domain contexts, conflicting evidence that cannot fit, restrictive audience transitions and unknown biography. Freeze a baseline/candidate corpus and maximum budget before running. Compare source-level precision/recall and required-counterevidence/exception recall separately; abstentions need correctness labels, and cannot automatically count as success.

No additional EXP14 production change is made by this audit. A general semantic fallback could improve recall while increasing unsupported-person risk; it requires gold-driven evaluation and preservation of v1/v2 release replay. Current source-backed lexical abstention remains an explicit tradeoff.
