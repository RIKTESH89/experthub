# Premium evidence sculpture — art direction and prototype acceptance

User explicitly requested complexity and a premium finish comparable in ambition to the researched studio work. The current prepared MeshBasicMaterial sheet/core renderer is a structural study only; it does not meet that finish. Do not present it as an approved premium prototype. The exact approved packages are now installed in the owned runtime; no asset-service spend has occurred.

## Original composition

Treat the hero's right panel as a small editorial sculpture photographed on a calm studio surface, with a distinctive forest ceramic core rather than a generic connected sphere. The core is a shallow sculpted medallion with concentric recessed ribs, an inset ExpertHub mark and one restrained satin-metal seam. Around it, five offset archival source fragments have curved corners, subtle bowed geometry, layered paper edges and engraved line marks. Unequal depth/scale and a strong diagonal arrangement create an intentional silhouette. A narrow source strip on one fragment receives a brief highlight to link the object with the HTML evidence explanation.

Ivory paper, forest ceramic and warm satin metal are separate roughness/material families. Use soft key, fill and rim lighting with controlled tone mapping; avoid radioactive mint bloom, mirror chrome, translucent glass and decorative particle clouds. Light theme sits on warm paper; dark theme preserves readable ivory fragments with a deeper tonal core and stronger rim separation. Native HTML headings, actions, AI disclosure and explanations remain in their current composition.

## Choreography

Three clear beats within five seconds: source fragments approach in an offset fan; a short orbit reveals layering and shallow relief; fragments align into an ordered answer folio beside the core. Use eased, staggered motion and a quiet final hold. Ordinary scroll may advance the same transformation, never hijack it. Fine-pointer parallax stays under 0.08 radians and does not restart idle animation. Source highlight is a conceptual illustration, not a live citation/retrieval trace, chain of thought or representation of actual model internals. Replay is explicit and keyboard-accessible.

## Complexity with restraint

Complexity comes from composition, geometry silhouette, material distinction, lighting and timed transformations—not high object counts. Prefer shared/instanced geometry for fragments and edge details. Desktop budget can expand deliberately to <=24 draw calls and <=30k triangles for the richer study, with no postprocessing or downloaded models/textures; measure real frame times before accepting any budget increase. Mobile uses three fragments, fewer paper layers, a simplified core and DPR 1. Its composition still needs to look crafted; it must not become an unrelated placeholder.

Keep the short animation, offscreen/background pause and static reduced-motion/WebGL/slow-device fallback contract. The static poster must depict the same sculptural arrangement, implemented from owned SVG/CSS or a locally captured final render after approval. No generic loading spinner or blank canvas. Reserve dimensions so enhancement adds no layout shift. The implemented prototype uses a 16-draw-call cap after observing 15 draws and 6,524 triangles on desktop; mobile uses three fragments.
