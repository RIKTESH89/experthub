The authored importer reads only OBJ vertices and faces. It ignores material references and other directives. The neutral clay contact sheet is `scaffold-inspection.png`; inspection scene is `scaffold-inspection.blend`. 36 hemisphere components total 64,159 vertices and 112,238 faces before weld. Coordinate extents approximately 141 × 180 × 117 mm. Meshes have raw split-vertex seams and are not assumed watertight; the adaptation welds vertices and records remaining boundaries.

Actual clay-frame verdict: usable anatomical scaffold. Both hemispheres and their deep midline read clearly; major frontal, temporal and superior fold hierarchy is considerably more convincing than the rejected procedural oval. Individual segmented gyri show some pillow-like interfaces and low-resolution detail, so this is not automatically suitable for a clinical or extreme macro render. No arbitrary new sculpted folds were added.

Adaptation modifications: duplicate seam vertices welded, normals recalculated, uniform scale/translation, original alabaster/paper studio materials, derived finite-thickness horizontal anatomical lamellae with portions omitted to make the twin visibly incomplete.
